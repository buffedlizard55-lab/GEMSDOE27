## 3. Why the deletion raises DTI (MODEL, from the official metric in `src/gems27/metric.py`)

DTI = TP / (0.2·TP + 0.2·FP + 0.8·|G|). At the 0.2477 operating point the denominator is
≈49 % false-positive mass (`knowledge/01`). Deleting a dot set raises DTI iff its credit
efficiency dTP/dFP < 0.2·DTI/(1−0.2·DTI) ≈ **0.052–0.059** at DTI 0.25–0.28. Dots 100–200 m
from a *known* fault are almost pure FP mass live, for three compounding reasons:

1. **Masking asymmetry.** Catalogue pixels are masked out of scoring; hidden truth is by
   construction *not* the catalogue. A dot hugging a known trace can only earn credit if a
   distinct hidden fault pixel lies within its 300 m kernel — geometrically unlikely when the
   dot exists because the detector fired on the known fault's own scarp/edge, rasterised
   1–2 px off the mapped trace (dip offset + 100 m quantisation).
2. **Detector echo.** The H19-5 family corroborates lines across topo/geophysics; its strongest
   responses are on mapped structures. The 100–200 m annulus is where the echo of a *known*
   fault lives. GEMSDOE27 measured the r≤1 version OOF (+0.0022 solo, +0.0141 stacked,
   `evidence/oof_hypothesis_gates.json`, HOLDOUT-DTI); GEMSDOE32 extended the radius to 2 px
   and live-mirrored +0.00487 in 4/4 folds (their site, HOLDOUT-DTI class).
3. **Budget interaction.** Dotting already moved the family to the flat optimum
   (44,090 px at d=2.8, MODEL 0.2550 vs 0.2477 live at 60,069 px). Removing the lowest-credit
   6–15 % of mass slides along the same curve: 0.2600 → 0.2708 (r1) → 0.2778 (B2)
   (OWNER-REPORTED). GEMSDOE36's independent PINN file (OWNER-REPORTED 0.2750, re-read here:
   38,854 dots, also **0 within 2 px**) converges on the same B2 + ~38 k optimum from unrelated
   physics — the strongest cross-method evidence that the optimum is real and emission-side.
