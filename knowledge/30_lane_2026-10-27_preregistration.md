## Frozen feature construction

Read the cached, label-free `det_elev` band from `data_cache/prepared/features.npy`. No labels, catalogue geometry, holdout masks, sibling raster predictions, DEM-flow direction or geothermometer information enter these features. Smooth with a Gaussian of sigma 2 pixels, normalized by a filtered finite-data mask. Derive grid-coordinate gradients and the slope magnitude; the unit normal is the direction of increasing *detrended* elevation. Sample slope at plus and minus 3 pixels along that normal with bilinear interpolation, and use `max(slope_plus - slope_minus, 0)` for asymmetric higher-/lower-side slope contrast. Compute positive curvature transverse to the same normal from the Gaussian Hessian. Normalize each nonnegative component by its 99th percentile within the footprint, clipping to [0,1], with constant/missing data returning zero. Fixed output columns are (1) the side-slope contrast, (2) transverse concavity, (3) their geometric mean, and (4) the minimum of that product at plus/minus 10 pixels in the local tangent direction. Outside-footprint samples are zero; all columns are finite within the footprint. The fourth feature is a conservative aligned-support proxy, not detection of actual fan outlets. No tuning or feature selection after outcomes.
