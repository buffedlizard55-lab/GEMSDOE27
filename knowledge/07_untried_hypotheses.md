# Next-session geological hypotheses (H28 series)

**Status:** prior-generated candidates, not yet validated unless explicitly stated. No weekly submission slot has been used for any H28 candidate. Rankings are expert priors, not measured leaderboard gains. Candidate novelty is established only against this checkout and the method descriptions available here; the owner-provided score list for sibling repositories does not expose their source code, so global novelty cannot be certified.

## Target and scoring frame

This competition predicts **fault locations** in a 100 m GeoDAWN raster, scored against newly mapped fault labels. Geothermal vents and wells are scientifically useful context, but they are not the target labels. The public `0.2477` and `0.3195` figures in this project are owner-reported; the 27GEMSDOE candidates are unscored. The agent does not access or automate DrivenData.

The current best in-repo spatially blocked research comparison is the 4-quadrant OOF detector plus T-v2 topology links and H27-4 flank-shadow pruning (`evidence/oof_hypothesis_gates.json`; seeds 130–139). Its mean paired DTI gain over the OOF base is +0.0141, 4/4 quadrants. This is an **internal-catalogue** test, not the competition's new expert-label test. The H28 top-candidate gate is preregistered separately in `knowledge/08_preregistration_H28-1.md` and must compare against that same OOF best, not against a weaker raw model.

## Ranking interpretation and stop rules

1. H28-1 is selected for the first confirmatory test because it uses an independent subsurface-structure signal and is testable using the already restored feature raster. This is a priority choice, not a claim that it will win.
2. H28-1 must beat the current OOF best under the preregistered paired spatial comparison in `knowledge/08`. If it fails, it is rejected for this slot; no threshold will be tuned against those folds. A later H28 idea requires a new preregistration and fresh seeds.
3. Even a positive catalogue holdout does not establish transfer to the organizers' newly expert-labelled faults. It may justify a controlled A/B submission candidate, but does not establish a public score, guarantee an improvement on 0.2477, or establish that 0.3195 is attainable.
4. The owner-supplied comparison list includes prior geophysics/ridge and alteration/magnetic scores. Their methods are not fully recoverable from this checkout; do not describe these H28 hypotheses as globally unprecedented.

## Sources verified for hypothesis design

- [USGS GeoDAWN ScienceBase record (Glen & Earney, 2024)](https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/657e1d85d34e23d3533209f7): official summary says the magnetic and radiometric survey supports geologic/geophysical mapping and subsurface structure studies; the page describes deliverables including geophysical grids.
- [USGS 3DEP 1-m DEM record](https://data.usgs.gov/datacatalog/data/USGS:77ae0551-c61e-4979-aedd-d797abdcde0e): official data-catalog record describes bare-earth 1-m DEM coverage and identifies 3DEP products as public domain. The local feature TIFF is an owner-repository mirror/derivative, not downloaded from USGS in this session.
- [INGENIOUS GDR #1391](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391): official page describes the regional compilation and lists seismicity, wells/springs, 2-m temperature and geophysical resources; the current sandbox could read the listing but could not retrieve the new ZIP binaries directly.
- [Berkowitz et al. (2000), publisher abstract](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/1999GL011241): supports ensemble-scale connectivity dependence on length exponent, fractal dimension and density. It explicitly does **not** claim that a particular segment decides a particular real network; the local graph test is our own, separate claim.

## H28-1 frozen holdout result (2026-10-02)

The pre-registered seeds 140–149 ran only after commit `c5d40b7` recorded the hypothesis and gate and commit `5a6972e` recorded the implementation. The gate **passed**: mean paired DTI `+0.00294884` across 40 cells versus the current OOF best (`B_oof + T-v2 + H27-4 r1`); the four-fold means improved in 3/4 folds and the ten seed means improved in 9/10. Baseline mean DTI was `0.10453780`; augmented H28-1 + T-v2 + H27-4 r1 was `0.10748664`. NE_LidarGapHeavy (`-0.00118`) and seed 149 (`-0.00301`) were negative; the gain is not uniform. Full per-cell evidence and data/code hashes are in `evidence/h28_1_edge_holdout.json`.

This is a catalogue-internal, spatially blocked component holdout, not a DrivenData score and not proof of transfer to the expert-created test labels. The transform is label-free, but its 1 km covariate smoothing uses inputs available across the existing 600 m fold buffer; spatial covariate correlation near fold boundaries remains. The runner wrote no submission TIFF and used no weekly submission slot. The result only clears the pre-registered gate for a future content-hashed research candidate; it does not establish a score increase or that 0.3195 is attainable.
