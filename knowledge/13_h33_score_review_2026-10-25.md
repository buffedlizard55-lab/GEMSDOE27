## What the file does, and what the available evidence supports

The published construction is **deletion-only**, not a new geothermal-vent detector: start from a 40,199-pixel owner-labelled 0.2708 base and remove every emitted dot whose pixel-centre distance to the mapped-fault catalogue is ≤2 cells (200 m), leaving 37,654 dots. The exact emitted raster has SHA-256 `c55bafc470054e8271dcb89347a17e07fefe50de6af6e6ba6c4b169ef7ab6fa9` for the zeros-outside file and `baeae3219bba6a19bc8cfe79224e28555c50184b7c1ca65c2a822f54807c77dd` for the NaN-outside file. Both have grid shape 3730×3292, EPSG:32611, 100 m pixels, one float32 band, 37,654 positive cells and no positives on the published catalogue, according to the owner-generated audit.

The H33 audit reports 2,545 removed pixels, a 4-quadrant +0.0048695 shift under its calibrated “live-mirror” instrument, a safety factor 2.0849, and projected DTI 0.274673. These are **calibrated model/holdout outputs**, not an organizer score. Its own catalogue-hidden mean for this candidate is 0.00460, and its documented strict off-catalogue calibration has known transfer limitations. No independent hidden-label truth or score receipt is included in those artifacts.

A plausible, bounded explanation for a true improvement is metric economics: under the competition's distance-weighted Tversky definition (α=0.2, β=0.8, triangular 300 m kernel), a prediction that contributes little to weighted true-positive credit can cost false-positive mass. Removing dots in a 200 m catalogue flank can improve the ratio if those dots mostly duplicate already-known structures or sit in low-probability areas. But it can also remove genuine uncatalogued faults close to mapped faults. The construction is a precision/false-positive calibration, **not evidence that the removed or retained locations are geothermal vents**.

**Therefore:** the available evidence explains a plausible mechanism for a gain, but does not prove why the user-reported 0.2778 occurred. It is possible in principle to exceed it, but no projection or catalogue-internal holdout result establishes that we can. Any future TIF must independently beat a preregistered same-run holdout baseline and pass the duplicate lane gate before it is eligible for a separate slot selector.
