## Integrity incident and recovery

The first execution completed the frozen E1 canaries/surface check and all E2 holdout cells but the E3 writer failed before persisting JSON: the template's full-width block width 3292 was passed with `tiled=True`; GDAL rejects this because TIFF tile dimensions must be multiples of 16. The first log, including rounded per-cell scores, is preserved at `evidence/h28_6_first_execution.log`; the incomplete `.partial.tif` was removed. The writer now writes an untiled GeoTIFF and the experiment runner saves an E1/E2 checkpoint before E3.

For recovery, **no model was refit and no feature, seed, threshold, or gate changed**. The deterministic canary/surface/DTI functions were replayed from the exact SHA-verified OOF probability caches. All 12 logged surface correlations matched exactly at five decimals, and all 40 baseline/candidate/random-control cell DTI values matched the first-run log at five decimals. This is evidence recovery, not independent confirmation or a fourth experiment. The discrepancy is recorded as IR-2026-10-25-07 in `registry/irregularities.json`.
