   - **Geophysical bottleneck in `NW` (`0.03804` vs `D0 = 0.03798`) and `SW` (`0.02872` vs `H29-S = 0.02967` and `H31-A = 0.02982`):** `NW` (Granite Springs, Kumiva, Smoke Creek, Black Rock) and `SW` (Carson Sink, Bradys, Desert Peak, Soda Lake, Salt Wells, Dixie Valley South) are dominated by **dipping (`50°–65°`) Cenozoic half-graben normal faults** whose conductive hanging-wall smectite clay caps (`cond_surf`) and basin fill (`depth_to_base_surf`) are offset `400–800 m` down-dip (`+\hat{\mathbf{u}}`) from resistive, high-density footwall horsts (`-\hat{\mathbf{u}}`), and whose range-front scarps between transverse canyons are buried beneath Holocene debris-flow fans. `H31-1` evaluated conductivity, gravity, and basement relief **isotropically at the same pixel**, confusing expansive flat conductive playas (`C(+\hat{\mathbf{u}}) - C(-\hat{\mathbf{u}}) \approx 0`) with dipping fault conduits and losing range-front continuity across buried inter-canyon fans.
