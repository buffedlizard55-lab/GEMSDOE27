### Split-integrity finding and fixed remedy

A pre-run audit with `PYTHONPATH=src` found that the historical `holdout.make_split` hides 8-connected pixels *after clipping them to a quadrant*: 39 connected components (1,371 positive pixels) cross quadrant boundaries, and seed 410 would leave pieces of several such components visible in some folds. More importantly, NBMG Qfaults vector attribution maps the 60,988 catalogue pixels to 413 distinct polyline records; **47 of those groups span multiple quadrants, accounting for 13,105 label pixels** (one group spans three). The local Qfaults file is `data_cache/qfaults_v2_in_footprint.json`, SHA-256 `4d6efc7bb3659ea2545353fcec574ef085b0acdb189c7590e4420a7c6c57b41c`; the existing vector-alignment diagnostic reports 98.42% of label pixels within 100 m and 99.9705% within 200 m of a Qfaults line.

Therefore this experiment will **not** use component-clipped `make_split` or `vector_graph.make_group_split` (the latter clips held groups to the quadrant). It will use shared group-aware holdout utilities added to `src/gems27/holdout.py`: (a) assign every positive label pixel belonging to one Qfaults vector-feature/FID group to the quadrant of that group's rounded label-pixel centroid; (b) within each assigned quadrant and seed, hide 20% of whole groups across the full grid; and (c) keep the whole hidden group out of training and visible-label masks. Background cells retain the original spatial quadrant. Tests must prove every positive group has exactly one fold and every selected group has zero visible pixels. The historical splitter and historical evidence remain unchanged and explicitly non-comparable.

## 2. Frozen feature definition

The data source is the hash-verified local 32-band stack (`data_cache/prepared/features.npy`, SHA-256 `83ed2704ee2de03cf8b1c8f2966fcf71813501df97c1c35400e6c0415393f6dc`) plus its pinned source rasters. The inspected metadata identifies band names but does **not** establish physical units. Consequently the new features are dimensionless rank/standardized proxies; do not describe them as measured susceptibility, density, magnetization, or alteration.

No labels, `existing_faults.tif`, catalogue distances, or hidden/visible group masks may enter feature construction. No feature may be fitted using labels. The only two frozen additions are:

1. **`h31_2_local_rtp_gravity_residual`** — rank-transform `rtp` and `iso_grav_anom_vg` independently over finite footprint cells to `[0,1]`. On a fixed `31 × 31` (3.1 km) footprint-normalized window, compute local means, variances, and covariance; predict the gravity rank by local least squares on the RTP rank; return the absolute residual divided by its local residual standard deviation, clipped to `[0,5]` and divided by 5. Non-finite or unsupported cells are zero. This is a local cross-field discordance statistic, **not** a literal Poisson/pseudogravity transform.
2. **`h31_2_gravity_edge_demag_interaction`** — `rank01(abs(iso_grav_anom_hg)) × (1 − rank01(abs(tmi_vg)))`, where each rank is computed over finite footprint cells. This is a predeclared interaction between horizontal gravity-edge strength and muted shallow magnetic vertical-gradient amplitude.

`TMI_up150`, `K`, and `Th` are deliberately excluded; adding them after results would be a new hypothesis. Test each of the two new channels individually as well as the full augmented model. All feature generation is label-free and occurs once; no per-fold tuning or feature selection is allowed.

## 3. Frozen model, split, metric, and gates

### E1 — leakage, surface, and split audit (stop here on failure)

- Load NBMG Qfaults attribution using the shared `vector_graph.load_vector_attribution`; use `pix_fid_idx` as the group map. Log alignment statistics, group counts, and cross-quadrant groups.
- Build the shared Qfaults-grouped quadrant map and assert each positive group maps to one fold. Construct the 40 registered split cells (10 seeds × 4 folds) with whole-group hiding and `frac = 0.20`.
- Compute direction-invariant ROC-AUC separately for **each** raw input feature in the current 79-channel H31-1 stack and each of the two H31-2 features, over the union of withheld positives and a deterministic sample of up to 250,000 valid negatives. AUC `> 0.90` for any channel is a leakage warning and fails E1; disclose every channel result. This canary does not establish predictive value.
- Fit the same shared H31-1 dual-regime, nested spatial OOF architecture twice on the same grouped folds and model seed `410`: baseline = existing H31-1 inputs; candidate = those inputs plus the two H31-2 features in both M_Sub and M_Full / Stage 2. No other hyperparameter changes. Use the H31-1 registered 600 m training buffer, feature assembly, negative sampling, and chunking; distinct cache tags and complete input hashes are required.
- Before placement, compare each stitched OOF probability surface with all 12 SHA-pinned entries in `registry/parallel_submission_registry.json` over finite footprint cells. If any absolute Spearman rank correlation is **`> 0.90`**, stop as duplicate/drift. Do not inspect or use organizer endpoints.

### E2 — confirmatory whole-FID hide-and-recover holdout

- Fresh seeds: **410–419 only**, 10 seeds × 4 centroid-assigned quadrants = **40 paired cells**. In each cell, hold out 20% of complete Qfaults vector-feature/FID groups assigned to that fold; use the deterministic shared seed rule `4242 + fold_id + 1000 × seed`.
- For each cell, hide complete groups from both the training labels and `known` mask. The trained OOF model for fold `f` has no training labels from any group assigned to `f` and applies the existing **6-pixel / 600 m spatial guard**. Candidate placement sees only the OOF surface, the fold footprint, and the pixel-exact visible-label mask; it must never see `hidden`, score-validity masks, or hidden-group locations.
- Score with `gems27.metric.dti_binary` and the established DTI definition: `alpha = 0.2`, `beta = 0.8`, Euclidean linear-triangular kernel radius `R = 3 px` (300 m); visible catalogue cells are masked pixel-exactly. Exclude the 600 m buffer ring from scoring, but retain withheld positive pixels as scoreable truth. Both arms use H31-1 CELF placement (`gamma = 1.35`, `min_sep = 2.8 px`, B2 `distance_to_visible > 2.0 px`, 37,654-dot full-map equivalent split by fold footprint share).
- **Comparator:** H31-S / H31-1 re-fit under this same whole-FID grouped evaluator. The historical `0.047302` H31-1 result used component-clipped quadrant holdouts and is **not numerically comparable** to this new evaluator; do not use it as the gate baseline.
- Label every absolute score `HOLDOUT-DTI` with evaluator version `gems27.metric.dti_binary v1 + NBMG-Qfaults-FID-grouped-quadrant-buffered-v1`, withheld-positive occurrence count, and a seed-bootstrap 95% CI. Report the 40-cell pooled DTI, 10 seed-level DTI values, 4 fold-level values, paired candidate-minus-baseline delta, and paired 95% CI (10,000 deterministic resamples of the 10 seeds).
- **E2 passes only if all hold:** (1) candidate pooled DTI exceeds the re-fitted H31-S baseline; (2) mean paired seed delta is at least `+0.0010`; (3) paired 95% CI lower bound is `> 0`; (4) candidate improves in at least 8/10 seeds and at least 3/4 fold-pooled comparisons; (5) all 40 cells, positive counts, predictions, and score values are finite and valid. Otherwise verdict is `negative` and no candidate TIFF is made.

### E3 — uniqueness, candidate artifact, machine validation (only after E1 and E2 pass)

- Use the exact candidate OOF surface and fixed H31-1 CELF/B2 emitter used in E2. On the full footprint with all visible training labels, produce exactly 37,654 binary dots. Before writing any artifact, compare the final dot mask with all 12 registry rasters and the available same-repository scored/H31-1 rasters. A directed fraction of candidate dots within Euclidean 3 px of any reference **`> 0.70`** fails the uniqueness gate; do not alter dots to evade it. Reconfirm surface-rank correlation `<= 0.90` against the pinned 12-peer registry.
- If unique, write a *new*, content-hashed all-finite zero-outside GeoTIFF using `gems27.submission.write_geotiff`, with a one-TIFF ZIP, note (≤140 characters), and independent `verify_geotiff`/download checks. Candidate is stored under `docs/downloads/research/` and listed as **downloadable for review only; NOT selected or authorized for a weekly submission slot**. No upload or live-score claim is permitted. `promote` means only “passed this registered research gate”; it does not mean submission-slot selection.
- If any gate fails, create no TIFF, set `verdict = "negative"`, record all metrics and the failure in the JSON run card, and stop.
