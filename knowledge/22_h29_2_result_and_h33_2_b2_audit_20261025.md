## What the reported 0.2778 does — and does not — establish

The GEMSDOE32 file `gemsdoe32-h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros.tif` is byte-authenticated in the pinned peer registry: SHA-256 `c55bafc470054e8271dcb89347a17e07fefe50de6af6e6ba6c4b169ef7ab6fa9`; it contains **37,654** positive cells on the competition grid. The attached **0.2778 is OWNER-REPORTED, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED**: no submission receipt was available, and this session did not access DrivenData.

The file's construction is consistent with a catalogue-flank deletion, not a new detector. The earlier D2.8 raster has 44,090 cells; the H27-4-r1 version has 40,199; H33-2-B2 has 37,654. The repository's earlier byte audit reconstructed `h27-4-r1 & (distance_to_catalogue > 2 px)` exactly, removing 2,545 additional dots within 200 m of the catalogue. Under the metric implementation, removing dots can improve DTI when their marginal credit/false-positive ratio is below `alpha × DTI / (1 − alpha × DTI)`; near-catalogue dots may be redundant for hidden-truth credit while remaining false-positive mass. That is a **mechanistic explanation consistent with the raster edit**, not an independent reproduction of the organizer's score or proof that the submitted file earned 0.2778. See `knowledge/12_why_02778_won.md` for the original pixel-level reconstruction and its model assumptions.

A separate content recheck (`evidence/peer_h33_2_raster_recheck_2026-10-25.json`) found H33-2-B2 to be an exact pixel subset of the pinned GEMSDOE25 D2.8, GEMSDOE28 H27-4 R1, GEMSDOE30 D2.8, GEMSDOE31 H27-4 and GEMSDOE36 Anderson rasters. Every H33-2-B2 dot is also within 3 Euclidean pixels of each of those placements; the corresponding fractions are approximately 99.88% for GEMSDOE28 H36-1/H38-1 and 99.98% for GEMSDOE33 H33D. GEMSDOE36 contains the same 37,654 dots plus 1,200 more. This makes those placements **non-independent raster replications**. The audit authenticates pixel content and overlap, not any score/file association.

### E3 — full-fit uniqueness stop

Before placement, maximum sampled surface Spearman correlation was **0.71008**, below the 0.90 limit (against the prior H29-1b D-refill raster). The 38,000-dot full-fit set was then checked against all 12 pinned peer rasters and in-repository candidate TIFFs. Maximum directed overlap within 3 Euclidean pixels was **79.5053%** against `gems27-h28-1-edge-coherence-plus-t-v2-h27-4-20261002-1113fba5f6cb-nan.tif`, above the 70% limit. The maximum overlap against the pinned peer registry alone was 45.39%, but the stricter in-repository comparison still triggers the registered duplicate stop.

Following the frozen rule, the process logged the duplicate and wrote **no** TIFF or ZIP. Accordingly, `raster_sha256` is `null`; footprint-NaN, [0,1], CRS, shape and transform validation are **not run**, not passed. The run card records that distinction explicitly.
