## 1. LANE L-2026-10-26 (single method paragraph for this session)

> **Dip-corrected geothermal alteration detector.** A spatially-blocked out-of-fold gradient-boosted detector trained ONLY on geothermal-relevant subsurface physics — multiscale potential-field edges (RTP magnetic + isostatic gravity: gradient magnitude, tilt/analytic-signal, cross-field coherence), depth-to-basement step edges, geodetic strain-rate/dilatation corridors, conductivity gradients, and radiometric K/Th/U alteration-front gradients — with every lidar-scarp/topography band and every catalogue-distance feature excluded by construction. At emission time, high-probability detector ridges that have a potential-field edge within a 2–6 px (200–600 m) annulus are **dip-shifted** to that edge (up-dip correction for a blind dipping fault); radiometric alteration fronts are added as independent emitters where they align with strain corridors. Emission is priority Poisson-disk at ≥2.8 px on the corrected ridges + alteration fronts, B3 catalogue exclusion (no dot within 300 m of visible fault), fixed 38,000-dot budget. Validation is hide-and-recover of whole catalogue segments with 600 m buffer, visible faults masked pixel-exactly, pooled DTI (α=0.2, β=0.8, 300 m triangular kernel, evaluator `gems27.metric` v1) + leakage canary (AUC≤0.90) on each new transform alone. Nothing outside this paragraph is in-lane; hybrid controls are labelled and never shipped.
