## 3. Why it failed (mechanism read)

1. **The residual is weak marginally.** All eight features' univariate AUCs on withheld
   positives are 0.505–0.530 (E1). Withheld *catalogue* faults are dominated by unaltered
   range-front structures for which H31-1's coherent magnetic+gravity channel already works; a
   mostly-quiet residual channel mostly dilutes that ranking.
2. **The named mimic dominates where it matters.** Per-fold deltas vs H31S: NW +0.0008, SW
   +0.0009, NE-lidar-gap −0.0033, **SE Walker Lane −0.0123**. The SE collapse is exactly the
   preregistered mimic geography: reversely magnetised Tertiary ash-flow tuffs around Tonopah/
   Mina and depth-decayed magnetics under thick basin fill produce large positive residuals
   without hydrothermal conduits, and the stacker spent CELF budget there.
3. **Stacking itself costs a little.** STK0 (identical stacker, no new features) is also
   slightly below the raw `p_s2` arm (CI spans zero). This repeats the Session-10 H31
   rank-ensemble lesson: re-ranking already-strong OOF surfaces through another model layer
   does not add holdout DTI in this regime.
4. **Proxy-limit caveat (cannot be resolved locally).** The catalogue-internal holdout cannot
   see genuinely *uncatalogued* demagnetized conduits, because its truth is catalogue pixels
   (`knowledge/10`). So this negative falsifies "demagnetization features improve catalogue
   hide-and-recover" — it does not prove the physical signature has no live value. Under the
   frozen protocol that distinction cannot rescue the candidate: no weekly slot may be spent on
   it from this evidence.
