## Candidate audit and selection

- **Selected for this session: H28-6.** It has the best testability-adjusted value: its local inputs exist, their hashes match the repository's pinned owner-mirror manifest, and the edge transform can be frozen and evaluated without using labels to make features. This is not a claim that it is most likely to win, and its feature quality is not assumed.
- **Not selected:** H28-5 is testable from an owner mirror but has an unresolved raw-package provenance gap and requires more QA. H27-16 has plausible upside, but cannot be validated until the actual official package bytes are obtained and checksum-verified. We do not call a listing or a sibling runner report proof that the bytes are available in this checkout.
- **H28-4 status correction:** `registry/next_hypotheses.json` calls seismicity fabric untried, but `src/gems27/subsurface_features.py` already creates `sub_seismic_fabric_ieq` and `sub_seismic_fabric_deq` structure-tensor coherence, and the Lane L-2026-10-25 A/D arms included the 15-transform stack. The standalone incremental contribution was not isolated, but the unchanged sigma-5 seismicity-coherence transform is not genuinely untried. It is excluded from this screen to avoid relabelling prior work.

## Frozen stop rules

Use no more than three experiments and stop by two hours. The selected H28-6 is tested only against the pre-existing H28-1 + T-v2 + H27-4 r1 spatial-OOF baseline. Each input and derived feature gets its own hidden-segment AUC canary; any direction-invariant ROC-AUC above 0.90 fails closed pending leakage investigation. Compute continuous-surface rank-correlation against every pinned registry raster before dot placement. If any correlation exceeds 0.90, stop. After emission, run the same pinned-registry correlation and directed Euclidean-3-pixel overlap check; >70% overlap with any one registry raster means duplicate and stop. A failed holdout may produce a clearly marked negative research artifact, but never an eligible weekly submission. No weekly slot is selected or spent by this screen.
