### 1.2 Three Critical Repository Irregularities Uncovered in Session 9
1. **`IR-2026-10-25-10` (`scripts/run_unique_holdout.py` hidden-target exclusion bug & front-page contradiction):**
   - `scripts/run_unique_holdout.py` line 99 computed `d_cat = distance_transform_edt(~labels)` using **all** `labels` (`sp.known | sp.hidden`) instead of `sp.known` (visible faults only). Because `FAR_D_PX = 3.0` and the DTI kernel radius is `3.0 px` (`300 m`), requiring `d_cat >= 3.0` banned every prediction within `300 m` of withheld truth `sp.hidden`, mathematically forcing `TPw = 0.0` and `HOLDOUT-DTI = 0.0` across all 20 cells.
   - Furthermore, `docs/index.html` and `docs/executive-summary.html` displayed a green `Unique submission TIF — ready to download` box for `gems27-unique-scatter-farfield-20261025-98e59bf8e3ce-allfinite.tif` directly above `NO TIFF IS APPROVED TO SUBMIT`, even though `src/gems27/readiness.py` rejected that Run Card on 6 independent machine checks and its submission note was truncated mid-word at 200 characters (exceeding the 140-character Run Card cap).
2. **`IR-2026-10-25-11` (`H29-1b` self-comparison bug in `scripts/run_build_h29_1b.py`):**
   - In `evidence/h29_1b_run_card.json` (lines 210–231), `H29-1b` achieved `0.6500` (`65.0% <= 70%`) 3-px dot overlap with `H28-1` and `<= 0.4799` across all 12 peer rasters and Slots 1–5; the reported `0.8255` maximum was against `H29-1` (`c24b6371179c`, its own un-deconflicted draft) because `run_build_h29_1b.py` included `H29-1` in `ref_paths`. However, `H29-1b` also reused selection seeds `210–214` and applied post-hoc mask deconfliction against `H28-1` rather than a self-contained geophysical lane.
3. **`IR-2026-10-25-12` (`valid_scoring | hidden` target-halo leak & kernel-zeroing in `run_lane_holdout.py`, `run_h29_1_holdout.py`, `run_dconfirm_holdout.py`):**
   - `holdout.buffered_scoring_mask(sp, foot)` excludes `ring = (d_hidden <= 6.0) & ~sp.hidden`. Passing `allowed = (valid_scoring | hidden) & (d_known > 2.0)` into `emit()` and `ridge_nms(prob, allowed)` zeroed `prob` across the `100–600 m` moat surrounding `sp.hidden` while leaving `sp.hidden` (`d == 0`) positive—turning every withheld truth pixel into an artificial local maximum and deleting the `0 < d < 3.0 px` (`100–300 m`) triangular tolerance kernel during scoring (which is why `FPw` was an exact `.0` integer in all 20 cells of those holdout JSONs).
   - **Template fix in Session 9:** `emit()` is made 100% blind to `sp.hidden` and `valid_scoring` (receiving only `fold_mask` and `visible_known = sp.known & (d(hidden) > 6.0)`), and we report both the protocol-exact full-kernel blind evaluator (`full_kernel_blind`) and the legacy `buffered_scoring_mask` evaluator (`buffered_std`).

## 3. Frozen Preregistration for Top Candidate `H30-1`

### 3.1 Method Lane & Physical Architecture
1. **Label-Free Feature Transform (`src/gems27/updip_relay_features.py`, 8 features):**
   - `updip_rtp_edge_advected`: $|\nabla G_{2.5}(\text{rtp})|$ advected up-dip by $\Delta(\mathbf{x}) \in [3.0, 5.5]\text{ px}$ along $-\nabla G_4(Z_{\text{base}})/\|\nabla G_4(Z_{\text{base}})\|$.
   - `updip_grav_edge_advected`: $|\nabla G_{2.5}(\text{iso\_grav\_anom})|$ advected up-dip by $\Delta(\mathbf{x}) \in [3.0, 5.5]\text{ px}$ along $-\nabla G_4(Z_{\text{base}})/\|\nabla G_4(Z_{\text{base}})\|$.
   - `updip_concordance_x_bstep`: $(\text{updip\_rtp} \cdot \text{updip\_grav} \cdot \text{rank}(|\nabla G_3(Z_{\text{base}})|))^{1/3}$.
   - `crossfield_strike_bridge_s6`: Along-strike pair geometric mean $\sqrt{E(\mathbf{x}+6\hat{\mathbf{s}})\cdot E(\mathbf{x}-6\hat{\mathbf{s}})}$ bridging $1.2\text{ km}$ sub-alluvial interruptions.
   - `listric_rollover_curv_x_bstep`: Down-dip profile curvature contrast $|\partial_{\hat{\mathbf{u}}}^2 G_3(\text{det\_elev}) - \partial_{\hat{\mathbf{u}}}^2 G_9(\text{det\_elev})| \times \text{rank}(|\nabla G_3(Z_{\text{base}})|)$.
   - `oblique_transfer_strike_rot`: Strike-rotation gradient $\sqrt{\|\nabla G_3(\cos 2\theta_{\text{mag}})\|^2 + \|\nabla G_3(\sin 2\theta_{\text{mag}})\|^2} \times (0.25 + 0.75\,\text{rank}(\text{geod\_shearrate}))$.
   - `hydrothermal_temp_upflow_x_bstep`: Quality-filtered station-max well/spring thermal excess $\Delta T = \text{clip}(T_c - 20^\circ\text{C}, 0, 200^\circ\text{C})$ ($0 < T_c \le 350^\circ\text{C}$) smoothed at $\sigma \in \{8, 20\}\text{ px}$ and crossed with basement step and electrical conductivity.
   - `hydrothermal_geotherm_upflow_grad`: Quality-filtered station-max quartz/chalcedony/cation geothermometer excess $\Delta T_{\text{chem}} = \text{clip}(\max T_{\text{geo}} - 50^\circ\text{C}, 0, 250^\circ\text{C})$ ($25 \le T_{\text{geo}} \le 350^\circ\text{C}$) value + gradient at $\sigma = 10\text{ px}$ crossed with basement step.
2. **Three-Habitat Structural Emission (`B_CORE = 23,200`, `B_TOT = 35,200`, `SCARP_TOP_K = 120,000`, `min_dist = 2.8 px`, `B2` collar $d(\text{visible\_known}) > 2.0\text{ px}$):**
   - **Habitat 1 (`T-v2` Visible-Fault Topological Relay Bridges):** Collinear $1\text{–}4\text{ km}$ forward-cone links ($z \ge 3$, mutual-deduplicated, `SPACING = 3 px`) derived strictly from `visible_known` faults (`sp.known & (d(hidden) > 6.0)` in holdout; `labels.tif` on full map) and masked by $d(\text{visible\_known}) > 2.0\text{ px}$.
   - **Habitat 2 (High-Confidence `H30-1` Hybrid Core Ridges, budget `B_CORE = 23,200` scaled by fold footprint share):** Greedy Poisson-disk (`min_dist = 2.8 px`) on `oof_detector.ridge_nms(prob_H30_hybrid, foot, sigma=1.0)` where `prob_H30_hybrid` is trained on the 55-feature stack (`32 base + 15 subsurface + 8 H30-1`).
   - **Habitat 3 (Sub-Alluvial Up-Dip & Hydrothermal Innovation Refill to `B_TOT = 35,200`):** Greedy Poisson-disk (`min_dist = 2.8 px`) on `(rH_all | rS_all) & emit_mask & (d(scarp_120k | hab12) > 3.2 px)` ranked by `score_H3 = 0.70 * prob_H30_hybrid + 0.30 * prob_H30_sub`, where `scarp_120k = top_k(prob_B, rB_all & r1, 120,000) | top_k(prob_D, rD_all & r1, 120,000)` excludes only the shallow-scarp ridge skeletons of baselines `B` and `D` without banning `prob_H30_hybrid`'s own subsurface/up-dip innovation ridges.
